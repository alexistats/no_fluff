Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ effective_date }}

NoFluff is run by {{ operator }}. The short version: we collect the little we need to run a workout tracker, we don't sell your data, and there are no ads or tracking. This page explains the details.

1. What we collect

  • Account details — your username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Your workout data — the routines, workouts, sets, reps, weights, notes, schedules, and progressions you record.
  • Your settings — things like which routines you've hidden, your reminder time and time-zone offset, and (if you enable it) the private token in your calendar-feed link.
  • Your own API key, if you choose to add one for AI generation — stored encrypted, and shown only as the last few characters.
  • Basic technical logs — like your IP address and request details, kept briefly to operate and secure the Service.

2. What we don't collect

No advertising or analytics trackers. No selling or renting your data. No location tracking (your time-zone offset is a number like "−240", not your location). We ask for only what the app needs to work.

3. Cookies and on-device storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in — there's no advertising or tracking cookie. NoFluff also stores some data in your browser on your own device (for offline logging and to save a set you're typing); that stays on your device and isn't used to track you.

4. How we use your data

To run the Service: to sign you in, save and show your workouts and progress, send password-reset emails, send workout reminders if you turn them on, and — when you use the AI generator — create a program from the inputs you provide. That's it.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use a few trusted providers to run NoFluff, and share only what each needs to do its job:

  • Hosting & database — our app and database run on cloud hosting providers that store your data on our behalf.
  • Email — when we send you a password reset or a reminder, your email address is handled by our email-sending provider.
  • AI generation — if you use the AI generator, the inputs you type (goal, equipment, schedule, notes) are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to create your program. Please don't put sensitive personal or health information in those fields.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the Service and its users.

6. Where your data is stored

Our providers may process and store data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. By using NoFluff you understand your data may be handled in other countries, under their laws.

7. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed, any API key you save is encrypted, and traffic is served over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is ever 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your workouts, logs, settings, and any stored key. Brief technical logs and backups may persist for a short time before rolling off.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your data in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Deletion — you can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time.
  • Reminders — turn workout reminder emails on or off in Settings at any time.
  • Calendar feed — regenerate or disable your feed link in the app; regenerating instantly revokes the old one.

To exercise any of these, use the app or contact us {% if contact_email %}at {{ contact_email }}{% else %}at the address published on the app{% endif %}. Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have additional rights, which we honor where they apply.

10. Children

NoFluff is not for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

11. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top. Significant changes will be made clear in the app.
